The findings that matter

Copy number and BAP1 phase: the Caris analytical export labels MTAP, CDKN2A and CDKN2B Deleted, but the supplied data do not calibrate absolute tumor copies or homogeneous homozygous MTAP loss. BAP1 is linked locally to the overrepresented C SNP allele: 21 C+deletion, 7 C+reference, 9 G+reference and 0 G+deletion paired-name observations. This does not establish loss of the other allele, somatic status or a second hit. Broader regional allele imbalance strengthens the observation without resolving those alternatives.

BAP1 antigen evidence: the mutation-bearing RNA and flanking annotated splicing survive the completed re-alignment. Conditional HLA-B*50:01-compatible RNA is detectable in bulk reads (2,132 paired names), but shared HLA markers and mixed-cell RNA prevent independent clinical typing, tumor-retention or surface-presentation claims. IEERKGLYL/EERKGLYL remain candidates for laboratory testing, with matched reference and nearby human-sequence controls. Binding predictions are not evidence that tumor cells naturally display the peptides.

RASA1 and sequence controls: the provisional class-II testing pair is mutant GDYYIGGRRFSSLQT versus reference GDYYIGGRRFSSLSD under provisional DPA1*01:03/DPB1*02:01. Both score highly in the controlled model; five of seven possible binding cores are unchanged reference sequence, so mutant specificity must be tested. No robust mutation-selective BAP1 class-II lead emerged. The sequence package also preserves unvalidated LATS1/LATS2 observations; LATS1 predicts an immediate stop with no novel tail, and LATS2 Gly960Arg has no established functional effect.

Full-reference RNA/fusions: all 23,209,264 original RNA pairs were analyzed. Review of 28 accepted and 26 selected discarded fusion hypotheses established no validated activating or treatment-selecting fusion. CEACAM3 noncoding and KRTAP9-1 repeat-associated signals have DNA support but no demonstrated driver interpretation. CCND1::HEG1 has low-count joined RNA evidence and two imperfect DNA hints; it is not a confirmed genomic fusion or drug target. The bounded MET exon-14 check detected normal junctions and positive controls but no canonical skipped junction; it does not clinically exclude all MET alterations.

Method limits stayed visible: the 45-case public fusion benchmark showed some support-count differences between sparse and dense compact indexes. It cannot estimate clinical sensitivity or a detection limit. The surviving trimmed pairs merge into longer junction-spanning sequences, so the experiment does not validate an effective 12-base anchor. Caller filters, repeats, overlapping mates and sparse indexing still qualify negative fusion conclusions.

Final alignment-method comparison

The full RNA library was re-aligned with a separate pipeline, preserving the fixed 4,015-site cohort. Of these sites, 3,932 retain depth 20 and 83 fall below it; none has zero qualifying bases. Among the original 2,873 DNA-dominant sites, 2,805 retain both depth 20 and at least 90% of the specified allele; 59 fall below depth 20 and 9 remain callable but below 90%. The depth-qualified 98% count is 2,721/2,873. No callable DNA-dominant site becomes at least 98% the opposite REF/ALT allele.

Five of six stronger baseline discrepancies persist. At APOD, 26 of 27 originally C-labelled names now contribute DNA-expected G; one fails matched filters. The new placement is not thereby proven correct. PABPC1 remains discrepant at 61/470 and 73/430; 61 names carry both bases on the same qualifying records. These shared observations are not independent, and origin remains unresolved. All 13 original exceptions and three MAPQ-sensitive flags remain documented; an additional MLLT3 site falls below 90% at depth 27, below the stronger screen.

Independent review reproduced all 4,015 rows, 49,911 integer comparisons and 989 name transitions. These are the same reads, not a new biological sample. Retention requires depth 20: the fraction-only count 2,860 includes 55 shallow sites and must not replace 2,805. Biological research priorities are unchanged.
