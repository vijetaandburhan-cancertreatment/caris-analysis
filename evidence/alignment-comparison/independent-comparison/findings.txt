Independent all-site and read-name audit — PASS

All 4,015 fixed baseline SNP sites remain represented. The independent aligned-pair walker matches 49,911 integer fields against the primary comparison, including all A/C/G/T counts, conflicts, depth and coverage-qualified retention. There are 3,932 sites with new depth at least 20 and 83 below 20; none has zero A/C/G/T depth. Among the unchanged 2,873 DNA-dominant baseline sites, 2,805 meet both new depth 20 and 90% same-allele retention, and 2,721 meet depth 20 and 98% retention. The 59 DNA-dominant sites below depth 20 remain in those denominators. Fraction-only counts 2,860/2,776 include shallow sites and are secondary, not substitutes for coverage-qualified results.

All 3,426 frozen query names were recovered, with both mate flags observed for every name in the new capture. The independent original-name recount covers 7,620 records and reproduces all 16 original A/C/G/T vectors. All 989 prespecified unexpected-supporter transitions agree with the primary classification. The separate MAPQ-only sites retain their explicitly frozen allele selection; an initial auditor selection mistake limited to those 3 sites was corrected and documented, without changing counts or thresholds.

PABPC1's paired RNA substitutions are supported on the same qualifying aligned record, not just through matching query names: all 65 original shared names and all 61 new shared names have such a record. In the new alignment there are 103 qualifying records carrying both 591G and 604C, representing 61 query names; 56 belong to the original shared set and 5 are newly shared. These records establish local linked RNA-base evidence under the count filters. They do not establish tumor specificity, independent molecules, RNA editing, a diagnosis or a therapeutic target.

The whole mapping pipeline changed: original STAR 2.7.8a used the recorded CTAT/GENCODE37 reference, two-pass Basic, right-flush indels, overlap minimum 12 and overlap mismatch fraction 0.1; the local STAR 2.7.11b run uses the primary-assembly D8 index, single-pass default, no indel flush, overlap minimum 10 and mismatch default 0.01, among other differences tabulated separately. Alignment changes cannot be attributed solely to sparse indexing or assumed to correct the original biological interpretation. Ordinary D/N-only records were not retained, so skip/deletion/nonACGT inventories are not complete whole-BAM comparisons. Root's auxiliary forward-offset tracing is separate from this independently verified same-record linkage analysis.
