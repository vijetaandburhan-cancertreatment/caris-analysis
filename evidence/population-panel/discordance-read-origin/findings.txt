READ-QUALITY AND ORIGIN AUDIT — 5 October 2026
Six primary population-panel discordances; three additional MAPQ60-only flags

The primary discrepancies have different technical contexts. COL5A2 is strongly dependent on aligned read starts. APOD, EXOC3 and the overlapping MSH3/DHFR locus have representative local gap-placement alternatives. The two adjacent PABPC1 discrepancies share support and remain unresolved. These observations do not establish that every discrepancy is an artifact, identify the true allele source, or support a clinical identity/contamination conclusion.

PRIMARY SIX LOCI

COL5A2, chr2:189043211. All 208 qualifying unexpected-A alignment observations, representing 116 query-name/read-group units, put that base at the aligned query start;190 also put it at stored query position0. The clean filter removes all 116 A names while retaining 183 G names. The eight selected A sequences fit ordinary COL5A2 transcripts at the same genomic position. This is strong end dependence; the cause of the end bias remains unknown.

APOD, chr3:195568834. All 37 qualifying C-bearing alignment observations (27 names) have a nearby 4-base insertion in a G-rich context. In the first-best nominated ordinary local alignments of eight selected distinct sequences, the queried C falls in an insertion in seven and moves to another nearby coordinate in one. The ordinary alternative can score below a source-CIGAR reference path containing the C allele (example 278 versus 283). This demonstrates local placement ambiguity, not a proven correction of the SNP or all 27 names. The clean result C24/G0 has lower depth; it is not resolution.

EXOC3, chr5:443259. All 37 qualifying A-bearing observations (22 names) have a21-base N operation in a GCG-rich repeat. The queried A moves to a matching reference A at chr5:443280 in each of the eight selected sequences' first-best ordinary alignments. The source-CIGAR path with A can fit better (example 310 versus ordinary292). Some expected-C reads are short/softclipped and have alternative repeat matches too. EXOC3 representatives hit explicit search caps. This is repeat/gap-placement ambiguity, not a preferred true origin or validated genotype change.

MSH3/DHFR, chr5:80654917. Of 189 qualifying G-bearing observations (104 names),147 contain a nearby 27-base N operation;42 contain the neighboring1446-base splice. All eight selected sequences move the queried G to matching reference chr5:80654908 in their first-best ordinary alignment. Both MSH3 and reverse-complement DHFR reference models occur. Original-splice-path models containing G can score higher (example 290 versus ordinary259). These are local placement alternatives, not confirmation of a different gene of origin or a corrected allele.

PABPC1, chr8:100705591 and chr8:100705604. Clean filtering reduces unexpected G from 65 to 9 names and unexpected C from 78 to 31, but does not eliminate either. Selected unexpected sequences retain their mismatches at the same PABPC1 coordinates in the best nominated ordinary transcript alignment; a cleaner different-locus origin was not established. All 65 G-at 591 names also support C-at 604 in the same qualifying alignment. The other13 C-at 604 names lack a jointly callable591. The sites share substantial support and cannot be treated as independent checks. Their biological and technical origin remains unresolved.

THREE SENSITIVITY-ONLY FLAGS

FANCD2 chr3:10049469, SDHAP2/MUC20-OT1 chr3:195677778 and FOXD4L4 chr9:65737280 are not extra primary-screen discordances. MAPQ60 removes the DNA alternate support while leaving RNA counts unchanged: FANCD2 DNA C713/T28 becomes C230/T0; SDHAP2/MUC20-OT1 A1472/G725 becomes A118/G0; FOXD4L4 G818/A225 becomes G260/A0.

The two selected FANCD2 DNA-T sequences (seven baseline names) match161 bases perfectly at the ordinary FANCD2P2 region, with the queried base at chr3:11871392. Substituting T into the original FANCD2 source-CIGAR path also produces a perfect fit, so that match alone cannot assign these reads to the pseudogene. The selected FANCD2 RNA-T sequences still align at FANCD2. The other two sensitivity-only sites have no established different-locus origin. These examples show allele-selective filtering and reference ambiguity, not evidence that the primary selection should be replaced.

SCOPE, DENOMINATORS AND VALIDATION

Indexed fetches inspected 29,135 site-overlapping alignment observations across nine loci. Some observations repeat across the adjacent PABPC1 sites. Baseline and clean A/C/G/T name counts independently reproduced the existing all-site CIGAR audit at every locus. All-fetched sequences, quality values, flags, CIGARs, strand and mate details are retained.

The deterministic origin-search subset contains114 per-site sequence-selection entries and 105 distinct full stored sequences overall. At each locus, selection was limited to eight unexpected-RNA, four expected-RNA and two unexpected-DNA distinct sequences, ranked by name support then lexical sequence. Selected unexpected-RNA name coverage is51/116 for COL5A2,16/27 APOD,9/22 EXOC3,21/104 MSH3/DHFR,22/65 and 23/78 for the PABPC1 sites. These are bounded representatives, not independent molecules or a complete origin classification of every supporting read.

Existing primary GRCh38 and GENCODE37 references were searched using disjoint19-base seeds plus a terminal seed, both orientations, followed by the previously verified affine full-query score.15,631 patient-representative windows were scored;17,664 includes controls. Five transcript seeds reached the1,000-hit cap. Seven patient query/reference combinations reached the500-window cap, all at EXOC3; the repeat control also reached caps. Alternate haplotypes, arbitrary unannotated splice paths, and seed-free gapped alternatives are not exhaustively covered. First-best tracebacks in repeats are not uniquely correct placements. Scores are relative alignment scores, not likelihoods or read-origin probabilities.

Source-CIGAR reference paths, including explicit expected/unexpected SNP substitutions, were retained as competing models. They can outscore the ordinary alternatives and inherit structure from the original alignment; they are not independent validation of that structure or allele. Seven normal-parent/edit/splice controls recovered their true windows and expected scores; the repeat control remained ambiguous. Root's separate dynamic-programming implementation reproduced all 670 exported score cases and seven terminal/internal gap controls exactly. This validates scoring arithmetic, not search completeness or clinical performance.

The original population-panel results and completed v2 cancer-research handoff remain unchanged. No new full patient alignment, full patient BAM scan, download, read upload or external contact was performed. Full per-locus counts, representative denominators, placements and interpretation limits are in findings.json and per-locus-findings.tsv.
