The public-fixture benchmark retained the same accepted/not-accepted decision across all 20 matched compact D1/D8 conditions (19 unique paired-input sets), but sparse indexing still changed the supporting evidence. Pair BCR-ABL1-28 was lost from D8 support in four comparisons: the intact 40-pair fixture, all 13 sequence-selected crossing pairs, support-3 selection 1, and support-5 selection 1. A preserved positive call or equal total support therefore does not establish equivalent read-level recovery.

The completed matrix contains 45 prespecified cases: 40 compact-reference runs, four full-reference D8 checks, and one declared comparison without Arriba's known-fusion recovery flag. All inputs and changes were frozen and independently reviewed before execution. The initial design, prospective amendments, and exact final manifest are preserved. The final v1.1 manifest SHA256 is 4abf92bfee2d29e0b68f60513f9a5083a99bacb1fb9ec84c4b10665a9808ca09.

The source pool was selected independently of caller survival. Direct alignment to the fixed GENCODE v37/GRCh38 spliced BCR::ABL1 construct identified 13 paired fragments with a uniquely placed junction-crossing mate, at most three substitutions and at least ten bases on each side. All 13 had distinct paired coordinate families and distinct full paired sequences. Original sequences and qualities were retained except in the explicitly identified trimming experiments. These remain samples from one synthetic public fixture, not independent biological replicates or proven independent molecules.

The four selected strong-source pairs supplied three accepted supporting names when untrimmed. Compact D1 and D8 each retained two names after the 12- and 20-base trimming conditions and three at 30 bases; pair12 was the missing supporting name at 12/20. The matched full-reference D8 comparison likewise changed from three untrimmed supporting names to two at 12 bases, losing pair12. The fourth selected source, pair74, is not equivalent to a fourth mapped/supporting fragment: it remained unmapped in the compact fixture despite substantial sequence-truth anchors. These are individual-read trimming conditions, not validated effective 12- or 20-base fragment anchors. The surviving pairs66/24 have complementary overlapping mates that STAR merges, restoring much longer junction arms: approximately 101/92 aligned bases for pair66 and 77/102 for pair24, with 19 additional clipped bases in pair24. Pair12 has only one crossing mate and actually retains a short arm after trimming; it loses support. Trimming also changes read length, base-quality positions, overlap and mate geometry; these observations cannot isolate a universal anchor-length threshold.

The unmodified shortest-minor-anchor pair54 is not a pure 13-base experiment: one mate has 13/137 bases around the construct junction, while the other has 64/86. The pair54-only compact cases produced no chimeric input in either index. The reason for every unrecovered sequence-valid fragment is not established; no individual filter mechanism is inferred solely from its absence.

The NEW full-reference D8 support_all run used 45 input pairs: 32 normal-parent controls plus all 13 sequence-selected source pairs. It recovered ten accepted supporting names. Compact D1 recovered ten and compact D8 nine from the identical input. The full-reference D8 ten are not the compact D1 ten: full D8 loses pair28 and gains pair18. Pair54 and pair74 are also absent from accepted support. Thus the full/compact comparison changes reference context and possible competing alignments, and equal counts conceal a support-identity swap. It is not a full-reference D1/D8 equivalence test.

The fixed zero-fusion control consisted of 32 newly generated, exon-spliced normal BCR and ABL1 parental fragments, with original public-fixture quality strings and complete construction provenance. It did not reuse non-spanning fragments from the fusion transcript as a purported negative. All three parental-control runs mapped all 32 pairs uniquely and yielded zero STAR chimeric reads. Stock Arriba terminates with exit1 when no split reads or discordant mates are available; no call table was produced or fabricated. The same explicitly validated terminal state occurred in four pair54-only cases: two dataset labels use the identical pair54-plus-parental input, each run on D1 and D8. They do not supply independent replicate evidence. Across the 45 planned cases, 38 produced ordinary caller tables and seven produced this no-chimeric-input state. The first strict-runner halt on the parental control was preserved, followed by a reviewed execution-only handling correction and one identical control retry. Other nonzero exits were not whitelisted.

Some low-support candidate rows were discarded with the source label in_vitro. This is a candidate-level algorithmic filter, not proof that an individual fragment is false or a causal per-read rejection explanation. In the predeclared single-pair4 comparison, the target remained filtered as in_vitro with and without the -k known-fusion recovery option. That one comparison does not establish that known-fusion priors are generally unimportant. The original prior stayed enabled for every other applicable run; no biological thresholds were changed to make a case pass.

These findings describe software behavior on small, correlated, selected synthetic mixtures. The 32-fragment normal background cannot calibrate confidence, e-values, recovery thresholds, RNA abundance or detection limits for a 23-million-pair patient library. Compact references lack most genomic competitors, and four full-reference checks address only their declared cases. BCR::ABL1 is a known fusion with favorable annotations; this fixture does not represent all novel, repeat-rich, noncoding, degraded or FFPE-associated fusions. No clinical sensitivity, specificity, limit of detection, patient false-negative probability, new treatment target or clinical reclassification follows from this benchmark.
