Independent priority review: four accepted RNA candidates
5 October 2026 | Research audit, not clinical variant classification

The two abundant self-events are corroborated by local DNA sequence, whereas NOTCH2NLC is dominated by nonspecific repeat sequence and CCND1::HEG1 is a small, antisense/tail-to-tail candidate. None of these results establishes a cancer driver, tumor-specific peptide, treatment target or somatic status.

What was independently checked
I rebuilt exact 25+25-nt junction markers from Arriba consensus and recounted the 13,495 selected raw paired reads using plain-string matching, separately from the original automaton implementation. All eight candidate/quality counts and mate/family counts matched the full-input audit. A separate agent then independently fetched the two DNA loci and reproduced all eight50-/100-nt Q20/Q30 DNA counts exactly (independent-DNA-spot-recount.json in the parent read-level-audit directory). The original audit scanned all 23,209,264 pairs; this independent recount verifies its selected-read evidence, not the completeness of the original whole-file selection. Reference-pattern index, coordinates, annotation and forward/reverse counts were checked against the primary-genome and GENCODE37 transcript audit. Only local indexed DNA intervals were fetched for CEACAM3 and KRTAP9-1; no whole-DNA rescan or new alignment was performed.

1. CEACAM3::CEACAM3 — reproducible noncoding self-event; DNA corroboration
Arriba places the joined RNA at chr19:41807383 -> chr19:41803759, plus to plus, type duplication. Its selected transcript ENST00000415495.5 stops at chr19:41807218-41807220: the first breakend is downstream in the 3'UTR and the second is intronic. The caller's stop-codon label therefore does NOT mean a novel truncating coding mutation. It means no translated fusion junction is predicted in this transcript model.
Exact 50-nt RNA marker:194 pairs at Q20 (158 orientation-normalized sequence families),157 at Q30. The marker has no exact reference-genome or GENCODE37-transcript occurrence. Its left25-nt anchor has one exact genomic hit at the named locus; the right anchor differs from the named reference by one base, so zero perfect right-anchor hits do not demonstrate uniqueness. The observed A at the fourth base of the right anchor corresponds to reference G at chr19:41803762, conditional on this breakpoint interpretation.
Local DNA contains this same 50-nt sequence in162 primary, MAPQ>=20, nonduplicate/QC-pass fragments at Q20,137 at Q30. Extending to50+50nt retains100/55 DNA fragments at Q20/Q30, with both sequencing orientations. This substantially strengthens the joined-sequence evidence and makes an RNA-only explanation inadequate. It does not establish that a simple3,625-bp tandem duplication is the unique genomic explanation; the acceptor is in repetitive sequence and alternative haplotypes/paralog alignments have not been exhaustively excluded. No matched normal or validated structural-variant test was available. No coding neoantigen or clinical use follows from this event.

2. KRTAP9-1::KRTAP9-1 — expressed27-bp insertion/repeat-length allele, not a validated cancer driver
Arriba calls chr17:41190374 -> chr17:41190348 an in-frame27-bp ITD in ENST00000398470.1. Importantly, its consensus also has a lowercase mismatch. I verified exact sequence equality between that RNA representation (the apparent duplication plus mismatch) and the dominant DNA representation: insertion of GCTGTGGGTCCAGCTGCTGCCAGCCTA after chr17:41190343. Do not split the lowercase mismatch out as an independently proven SNV, or describe this as an exact reference-sequence duplication without qualification.
Exact50-nt RNA marker:518 pairs at Q20 (387 orientation-normalized sequence families),480 at Q30. Neither orientation occurs exactly in the primary genome or GENCODE37 transcripts. Local DNA has the same50-nt marker in731/675 qualifying fragments at Q20/Q30; the100-nt marker remains in329/262. The dominant27I CIGAR representation appears in325 named fragments;299 have a supporting read with exact10-bp flanks and BQ>=20 across the insertion and flanks. These are sequence-support counts, not a VAF.
The selected coding model remains in-frame, with9 additional amino acids and no new internal stop in this local model. Because the protein sequence is also repetitive, multiple equivalent positional descriptions exist; no clinical HGVS protein allele is asserted. Coordinate-anchored short-haplotype counting produced conflicting mate classifications in73 fragments with10-bp flanks and68 with20-bp flanks. That exposes local alignment ambiguity and prevents an uncomplicated alternate/reference fraction. Normal repeat-length polymorphism, paralog/haplotype differences and somatic change are not distinguished without matched-normal and orthogonal confirmation. No biological or therapeutic significance is established.

3. NOTCH2NLC::NOTCH2NLC — exact marker is not junction-specific
The caller's chr1:149390834 ->149390805 in-frame30-bp ITD lies in a near-pure CGG repeat; its assembled sequence also differs at one base from the named reference. The 25+25-nt marker is only repeat sequence. The raw1217 Q20 pairs (978 Q30) cannot be interpreted as independent evidence for this specific event: that exact50-mer or its reverse complement occurs14 times in the primary genome and10 times in GENCODE37 transcripts (including CARM1, AR and HMGB3). Both25-nt anchors have hundreds of matches. Do not claim a precise repeat expansion, NOTCH pathway activation, cancer-driving fusion, or tumor-specific peptide from these counts. We did not run a dedicated repeat-expansion or long-read assay.

4. CCND1::HEG1 — small candidate in an orientation that does not encode a conventional CCND1-HEG1 fusion protein
Arriba calls chr11:69651217 -> chr3:125013572 a translocation/3'-3' event. CCND1 is transcribed antisense relative to its annotated gene; HEG1 is sense. The reading-frame and peptide columns are absent. The official output definition explains that head-to-head/tail-to-tail orientation cannot yield a conventional chimeric protein with both annotated genes in sense orientation. A CCND1 gene name is therefore insufficient to infer a cyclin-D1 oncogenic protein or treatment target.
Raw exact evidence decreases from197 pairs for15+15nt at Q20 to11 for20+20nt and remains11 for25+25nt (7 sequence families;9 pairs at Q30). This large short-marker drop is an empirical warning about low-complexity context. Both25-nt arms map to their named reference loci and the joined50-mer has no exact primary-genome/GENCODE37-transcript match, so a small junction-sequence candidate is preserved for review. It is not classified as a driver or dismissed as certainly false. Independent DNA-breakpoint/RT-PCR confirmation and assessment of antisense transcription, library chimera and paralog alternatives would be needed before elevating it.

Shared limits and interpretation
All RNA sequence markers derive from caller consensus, so the raw-read check is corroboration, not independent biological validation. Exact-reference absence does not exclude mismatched/gapped paralogs, normal unrepresented alleles or unannotated transcripts. DNA counts collapse query name+read group, excluding unmapped/secondary/supplementary/QC-fail/duplicate-flagged reads and requiring MAPQ>=20; marker bases all meet the stated quality cutoff. Whole-read sequence families and paired alignment descriptors are descriptive, not UMI-defined original molecules. No matched normal was used. No RNA/DNA allele fraction is claimed from these selected counts. A research-supported sequence is distinct from a clinical pathogenicity or actionability conclusion.
