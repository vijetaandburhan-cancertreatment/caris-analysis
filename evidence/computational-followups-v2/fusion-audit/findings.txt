Independent raw-read fusion audit — research use only
Status: primary/supplemental raw-read and reference audits complete; exact DNA sequence evidence for CEACAM3 and KRTAP9-1 independently reproduced. The independent four-candidate priority review is final and incorporated; structural/normal-context interpretation remains qualified.

The local full-reference D8 STAR/Arriba run produced 28 accepted hypotheses (1 high, 17 medium, 10 low), not 28 established cancer fusions. The predeclared rules selected those 28 plus 26 of 825,436 discarded rows. No validated activating or treatment-selecting fusion has been established by this read audit. There are well-supported sequence junctions worth retaining, but function, tumor specificity and the underlying mechanism often remain unresolved.

What was verified
- All 23,209,264 raw RNA FASTQ pairs were scanned twice without alignment: first for simple caller consensus junctions and original read names, then for accepted one-base insertion consensuses and conservative reference-only hypotheses for the 26 selected discarded rows and ambiguous MADCAM1 call. Both runs reached normal paired EOF with matching pair names and sequence/quality lengths.
- The primary pass recovered all 913 distinct caller-named pairs and selected 13,495 pairs. The supplemental pass selected 320 pairs. These extractions can overlap and must not be added as independent evidence.
- Markers span 15, 20 or 25 bases on EACH side of a junction; all marker bases, including a non-template insertion when present, must meet Q20 or Q30. Both orientations and both mates are checked, and each query-name pair is counted once per candidate/arm/quality threshold.
- A separate gzip four-line FASTQ parser and exhaustive str.find implementation reproduced all primary and supplemental pair counts, named subsets and full-pair sequence-family counts. This is a separate implementation on the extracted positive set; it is not a second exhaustive search of omitted-negative raw pairs.
- Exact junctions and each constituent anchor were searched against all 3,099,750,718 bases of the local GRCh38 primary assembly and 234,485 GENCODE37 normal transcripts (384,229,184 bases). Public matcher, overlap and chunk-coordinate regressions passed. This is exact-reference matching, not exhaustive gapped/mismatched mapping or a search of every human haplotype.
- The public BCR::ABL1 control retained the known fusion, but strict thresholds lost true-positive support: only 5 raw pairs met15+15/Q20, 3 met20+20/Q20, 2 met25+25/Q20, and none met20+20 or25+25/Q30. Therefore zero exact/Q30 support is not an exclusion criterion.

Selected findings; counts are all-raw exact 25+25/Q20 query-name pairs / distinct full read-pair sequence families unless stated otherwise
- CEACAM3 self-junction: 194 / 158 (157 pairs atQ30). The caller links a3prime UTR to intronic sequence and provides no peptide. In selected ENST00000415495.5, the donor is163bp beyond the stop-codon end; the caller stop-codon label does not identify a new truncating coding mutation. Abundant raw support does not turn this into an intergenic driver or coding neoantigen. Independent bounded DNA audit and a separate recount confirm100 primary/nonduplicate MAPQ20 DNA fragments with the exact100nt junction atQ20,55 atQ30. This supports local DNA sequence, but does not prove unique structural interpretation, somatic status or a functional effect.
- KRTAP9-1 self-ITD hypothesis: 518 / 387 (480 pairs atQ30). It is a repeat-rich in-frame event with a9-amino-acid addition in the selected reference model. The lowercase mismatch and duplication form one equivalent insertion representation, not independently established lesions. Independent bounded DNA review found a27-bp insertion representation after chr17:41190343 (GCTGTGGGTCCAGCTGCTGCCAGCCTA), equivalent to the caller duplication plus local mismatch. A separate recount confirmed329 DNA fragments with an exact100nt marker atQ20,262 atQ30. Coordinate-aligned reference/alternate mate conflicts in the repeat make a reliable VAF inappropriate; germline/tumor specificity remains unknown. Do not label it a novel therapeutic fusion.
- NOTCH2NLC self-ITD: the exact50nt junction has14 primary-genome occurrences and10 total normal-transcript occurrences (9 transcript-pattern matches in the primary table; one transcript contains two occurrences). Thousands of raw matches to shorter markers are not specific evidence for this gene or ITD. It remains repeat-confounded.
- CCND1::HEG1: 11 / 7 (9Q30 pairs); the source assignment has 3prime-3prime geometry and no peptide. This supports an RNA sequence junction, not a conventional activating CCND1 fusion, amplification or drug indication. The15+15/Q20 count197 falls to11 with20+20, illustrating short-repeat-anchor inflation.
- LZTS2::CCND1: 5 / 4, out-of-frame. This is a low-support RNA junction of uncertain mechanism, not a demonstrated functional CCND1 rearrangement.
- FOCAD::MIR3681HG: the main out-of-frame RNA junction has5 pairs at20+20/Q20,4 at25+25; the latter all place the50nt marker only1nt from a read end. Two alternate junctions have one pair each. A genomic event, trans-splicing and a library chimera cannot be distinguished here; no full-length protein or vaccine target is established.
- AGAP3::HOXD11:22 /19 but its short predicted in-frame peptide and junction are highly GC/repeat-rich. A genomic fusion versus repeat placement/length or library explanation remains unresolved. Unique25nt anchors alone do not prove a tumor-specific protein.
- Several gene-pair labels share exactly the same canonical marker: the two chr15 insertion rows, all three ACE partner rows, two TUBB8P5 labels and two EP400 labels. They are not separate independent events. Some partner anchors have tens or hundreds of thousands of genomic matches.
- DMPK::ATXN3's40nt junction matches normal ATXN3 transcripts and genome. The EP400-labelled40nt junction has a normal genome match. Longer markers differ from nearby normal sequence by repeat/SNV context; raw count alone does not establish a fusion. TBP::ATXN3 is likewise poly-CAG/repeat-confounded.
- All26 selected discarded rows received a joined-genome-sequence hypothesis and nearby STAR-breakend-name review. Some MET/NTRK2/BRAF self-junctions and RET/ALK inversions are raw-supported, but the former are back-splice/noncanonical hypotheses and the latter have3prime-3prime or5prime-5prime geometry. They are not demonstrated activating kinase fusions. This analysis is not an exon-skipping test.
- The four discarded BAP1 partner hypotheses give0–4 strict50nt/Q20 pairs, often with repeat, short-anchor or two-family limits. None establishes a BAP1 structural second hit or biallelic inactivation. Two discarded rows outside BAP1 have no nearby STAR names under the fixed±25bp rule; reference-only counts do not recreate an absent caller consensus.

Interpretation limits
- A raw junction can be real RNA, and even have local DNA sequence support, without being a tumor-specific DNA rearrangement, full-length functional protein, driver, antigen or treatment target. No matched normal or orthogonal assay is present. Bulk RNA does not establish which cell type expresses it.
- A full read-pair sequence family is a sequence-diversity count, not an independent molecule. PCR copies can have errors, trimming or fragment-end variation; there are no UMI-defined molecule counts here.
- All28 accepted rows have zero caller discordant-mate support. Short inserts/overlapping paired RNA reads can explain this, so it is not by itself disqualifying; nevertheless no independent discordant-fragment support is established by this output.
- The caller omits assembled sequence and read identifiers for the selected discarded rows. Supplemental names are STAR alignments with the same genomic breakend directions within25bp, not Arriba's post-filter support list. Reference-only marker absence cannot exclude SNPs, splice changes, insertion or alternative consensus.
- The compact D1/D8 control comparison was not equivalent: D8 lost one BCR::ABL1-supporting read. The full-reference D8 control recovered the same total but a different supporting-name set. No validated patient sensitivity bound or comprehensive negative claim follows.
- The103,932 ignored-record warning is under source-level instrumentation. It combines wrong-end clipped supplementary RECORDS and rejected query-name+HI GROUPS, not necessarily unique reads or parse corruption. The separate fatal htslib parse-error route did not fire. These rejections may affect sensitivity; they are not established as harmless. Some loci also exceeded Arriba's default support subsampling threshold.
- Arriba does not call intragenic deletions. This analysis therefore cannot exclude deletion-mediated events, exon skipping, other unselected fusions or somatic structural variants.

Verified diagnostic addendum — 5 October 2026
This supersedes the earlier pending warning-counter status above. A separate CSV/field comparison verified that all28 accepted calls are byte-identical between the stock and counter-only rerun. Both discarded files contain825,436 rows:825,433 have identical fields; the only3 differences reorder equal-distance gene1 labels (FAM95B1/BX664727.3 in two rows and OOSP4A/AP000790.1 in one). The same labels and multiplicities remain. Breakpoints, read counts, filters, sequences, read identifiers and all other fields are unchanged. Thus the discarded files are semantically equivalent for these calls, but are NOT byte-identical. The original frozen call files were not modified.

Independent log parsing reproduces103,932 =61,161 rejected paired alignment groups of unexpected size +5,303 paired groups with unresolved split/supplementary overlap +37,468 individual supplementary records clipped at the wrong end. The first two are alignment-group counts; the last is a record count. One biological fragment may contribute more than once across hits/stages. Do not turn this sum into unique reads, molecules, a percentage of input lost or a sensitivity estimate. No fatal BAM-decoder error occurred; this warning is not evidence of that parse-corruption failure. It also does not prove that all rejected evidence was harmless. Subsampling counters describe cap-branch encounters/distinct fusion keys, not exact numbers of skipped original molecules or transcript abundance.

The counter rerun explains existing filters and preserved the reported findings; it does not rescue rejected evidence, validate tumor specificity, cure sparse-index sensitivity or exclude all fusions/deletions/exon-skipping. The compact D1/D8 difference and intragenic-deletion limitation remain.
