CCND1::HEG1 — bounded read-pair and alternative-mapping audit
5 October 2026 | Research result; independent audit completed

The joined cDNA sequence remains supported after mismatch/gap-aware reference review. I did not find a clean ordinary reference alignment that explains the junction-bearing reads as well as the proposed joined sequence within this prespecified search. The biological explanation remains unresolved: this does not establish a tumor DNA rearrangement, a cancer-driving fusion, or a treatment target. The antisense/tail-to-tail orientation,17bp microhomology and overlapping/repeated reads leave a library-generated chimera plausible.

What changed from the first audit
The original evidence was11 exact50nt Q20 read pairs (7 whole-pair sequence families),9 pairs at Q30, with no exact full-junction primary-genome/GENCODE37 match. This followup tested mismatched/gapped alternatives using the full read sequences and both mates. It examined the union of13 caller-named pairs plus1 additional exact-marker pair:14 pairs,10 whole-pair sequence families and18 distinct mate sequences of77-161nt. The extra three pairs do not meet the Q20 exact-marker threshold; they were retained as context, not promoted into the11-pair count.

Alternative-reference search
The scan covered all3,099,750,718 bases of194 primary-genome contigs and all384,229,184 bases of234,485 GENCODE37 transcripts. It searched multiple disjoint19nt seeds plus a terminal seed in both orientations, then scored nominated windows with the same fixed-window affine scheme used for the proposed junction models. There were224 distinct seed patterns and20,774 scored windows across patient queries and controls. The genomic and transcript-based constructions produced the same750nt proposed sequence; these are one hypothesis, not two independent confirmations. No seed-hit or window cap was reached. Peak recorded RSS was about69MB; no large index, full-patient realignment, full raw-RNA rescan or new upload was needed.

Among the13 distinct mate sequences belonging to Q20-support pairs, the proposed joined model exceeded the best nominated ordinary reference score by68-80 score units. These are relative algorithmic scores, not MAPQ, likelihoods or biological probabilities. The best alternatives were normal HEG1 genomic/transcript sequences, with multiple mismatches and internal gaps in the short segment attributed to antisense CCND1. For example, an exact90nt read scores180 against the proposed junction and111 against the best normal-HEG1 window; an exact118nt read scores236 versus167. A150nt mate shared by two lower-quality pairs maps perfectly to ordinary HEG1 and to the HEG1-only portion of the joined model, so that mate correctly supplies no fusion-crossing evidence.

This is not exhaustive gapped mapping. A candidate must retain a searched exact19nt block and fall within the20nt placement slop. The supplied primary reference assembly and GENCODE37 isoforms were searched, but unrepresented alleles, other genomic haplotypes, sufficiently mismatched/gapped paralogs and unannotated splice isoforms can still be missed. A superior joined-model score narrows a simple mapping-error explanation; it cannot distinguish a real biological RNA from a chimeric cDNA created during preparation.

Read geometry and breakpoint ambiguity
Ten of the11 Q20-support pairs have completely reverse-complementary mate sequences. Both mates therefore largely observe the same insert, rather than providing independent molecular support. One remaining Q20 pair differs by a single mate base. These11 pairs yield7 sequence families and6 start/end patterns on the proposed RNA model. Without UMIs, neither number establishes how many original molecules existed. Read names, repeated sequencing, PCR copies and overlapping mates must not be summed as independent biological observations.

Direct reconstruction of the joined reference sequence found17bp of shared sequence, TCCTCCTCCTCCTCTTC, and18 equivalent breakpoint representations. The stated chr11:69651217/chr3:125013572 breakpoints are therefore not uniquely localized within that tract. This independently agrees with the17-base repeat reported in STAR's chimeric records. Pair orientation is coherent on the proposed transcript, but coherent geometry can also follow amplification of an early library chimera. The long-mate pairs and conditional insert spans are saved without treating them as DNA insert-size measurements.

Controls and scoring transparency
Ordinary CCND1 and HEG1 parent controls, a canonical MET splice control and an annotated INS-IGF2 transcript control mapped as expected. A HEG1 control carrying3 substitutions scored304 instead of322; a1nt insertion scored316; a2nt deletion scored311. Their true parent windows were nominated. A synthetic proposed-junction control scored322 on its intended model and148 on the best ordinary transcript alternative. A separate50nt repeat control recovered all14 primary-genome and10 transcript occurrences at the perfect score100, demonstrating that exact support can be nonunique. This last short-repeat control was added transparently after the initial161nt repeat control had no perfect full-length reference placement; patient results were not changed.

Scoring uses Biopython1.86 global alignment: match+2, mismatch-4; internal gap oflengthk costs-6-(k-1); reference terminal overhangs are free; query end-insertion penalties are-2/base only at physical reference-window edges. The latter is NOT universal soft clipping after arbitrary free reference prefixes. That distinction was found and documented before patient scoring, with unchanged numeric settings; all coordinates, mismatches with original qualities, gaps and aligned fractions are retained for independent rescoring. A favorable short or partially aligned hit is not treated as a complete explanation.

Independent review
A separate standard-library dynamic program reproduced54 selected best patient/control proposed and alternative scores exactly. Six hand-calculable cases and300 deterministic A/C/G/T toy comparisons agreed with the frozen library conventions. Independent reference reconstruction confirmed the750nt model and17bp/18-representation breakpoint ambiguity. Separate sequence/quality checks reproduced11Q20/9Q30 pairs,10 fully complementary mate pairs,7 sequence families and6 start/end patterns. The independent reviewer did not repeat the complete nomination scan or rerank all20,774 windows. Its exploratory earlier end-gap implementations are preserved for transparency but are not the patient scoring method. Detailed evidence is in independent-root/findings.txt and companion files.

What would distinguish the remaining possibilities
The current library alone cannot establish whether the joined sequence was present as native RNA before reverse transcription. An independently prepared assay/library or a method not dependent on the same reverse-transcription mechanism would address that question; convincing DNA breakpoint evidence would address a different question, a genomic rearrangement. Neither is present in this focused audit. No matched-normal comparison was performed. Given the antisense3'-3' configuration and lack of a conventional fusion-protein prediction, this result should remain a low-priority unresolved research candidate unless additional evidence changes its significance.

Primary evidence for the mechanism caveat
Houseley and Tollervey experimentally generated reproducible noncanonical and sense-antisense fusion products during reverse transcription. This establishes a possible mechanism, not that it occurred in this specimen: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0012271
