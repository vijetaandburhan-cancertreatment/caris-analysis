Clinical interpretation limit
This is a focused research negative for the canonical exon13-to15 junction. It neither rules out every MET abnormality nor creates a new treatment recommendation. Even a low-level splice signal alone would require context, orthogonal confirmation and clinical molecular interpretation before treating it as a pathogenic genomic event.
