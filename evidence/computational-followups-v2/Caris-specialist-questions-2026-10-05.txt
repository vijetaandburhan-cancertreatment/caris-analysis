Five specialist questions | 5 October 2026

1. Can Caris provide its calibrated allele-specific CN/LOH segments, purity/ploidy and assay interpretation for MTAP/CDKN2A/B, and does the MTAP result meet its validated definition of homozygous tumor loss?
Why ask: The export says Deleted with MTAP CNA 0.80; our tumor-only analysis cannot translate this into integer tumor copies or homogeneous loss.
What the answer changes: Whether MTAP-loss trial screening has a sufficiently established biomarker, or whether orthogonal tissue confirmation is needed before relying on that status. Trial eligibility still depends on the specific protocol and clinical criteria.

2. Does BAP1 have a clinically confirmed somatic second hit or loss of the WT allele, and which matched-normal/allele-specific assay can resolve it?
Why ask: The frameshift is locally linked to the overrepresented C SNP allele, but 7 C+reference and 9 G+reference fragments remain. The current evidence cannot distinguish copy-neutral LOH, copy loss, allele-specific gain or mixed-cell explanations.
What the answer changes: Whether we can describe BAP1 as biallelically inactivated, how to interpret the mutation's tumor specificity, and whether genetics evaluation is indicated. Local phase alone should not decide diagnosis or treatment.

3. Can the research team test whether the BAP1 IEERKGLYL/EERKGLYL peptides are endogenously presented on HLA-B*50:01 and recognized selectively by T cells?
Why ask: Their coding sequences are supported in DNA/RNA and predicted binding is favorable, but no natural ligand or functional T-cell evidence exists. The package supplies WT counterparts and all 31 nearby reference-sequence controls; several predict stronger HLA binding.
What the answer changes: Whether to prioritize this sequence for vaccine/TCR research, retain it as a hypothesis, or deprioritize it after an adequately sensitive negative assay. A peptide-pulsing result alone would not establish endogenous tumor presentation.

4. For RASA1 GDYYIGGRRFSSLQT with provisional DPA1*01:03/DPB1*02:01, is a CD4 response selective for the mutation when tested against WT GDYYIGGRRFSSLSD, and which core/register is recognized?
Why ask: The controlled model assigns high EL scores to both mutant and WT. Five of seven possible nine-amino-acid cores are unchanged WT; the actual bound register is unknown. Confirm normal-blood HLA before committing to allele-specific assays.
What the answer changes: Whether RASA1 adds a distinct mutation-selective CD4 research target or mainly a shared-reference response. Model scores alone do not justify calling it a validated neoantigen.

5. Can LATS1 c.378dup and LATS2 c.2878G>A be confirmed in an independent tumor preparation and matched normal, then tested for loss of function/Hippo-pathway dependence if confirmed?
Why ask: The two candidates are absent from the supplied variant tables but have reproducible local read support. LATS1 predicts an immediate stop at residue 127 without a novel tail; LATS2 Gly960Arg remains of unknown function.
What the answer changes: Whether they support a stronger Hippo/TEAD research rationale or should remain unvalidated observations. A sequence finding alone does not establish drug sensitivity or trial eligibility.
