BAP1 antigen evidence: independent computational audit
1 October 2026

Result: no concrete sequence or affinity-table error found. The interpretation
is suitable for experimental triage, with the provenance labels below corrected.
This is a separate implementation checking the same data and public references,
not independent clinical validation.

Sequence checks passed. Direct reconstruction from NM_004656.3 confirms the
BAP1 deletion, first changed residue 57, seven-residue altered tail KGLYLGG,
and stop after residue 63. IEERKGLYL spans residues 53–61 and EERKGLYL spans
54–61; their position-matched wild-type sequences are IEERRSRRK and EERRSRRK.
Both leave two real G residues before the stop. The supplied natural local
contexts and sequence hashes agree. RASA1 independently reproduces the two-
residue QT tail and stop after residue 251.

Normal-reference checks passed. An independent unseeded scan tested every
equal-length window in 104,760 GENCODE37 proteins (40,063,142 residues). It
exactly reproduced every occurrence in the production seed-based search: no
zero-, one-, or two-substitution neighbor for the nine-mer; no zero- or one-
substitution neighbor for the eight-mer, but 24 distinct two-substitution
neighbors across 68 reference occurrences. This bounded comparison does not
cover population polymorphisms, unannotated ORFs, length-changing recognition
or arbitrary TCR cross-reactivity, and therefore is not a safety test.

Affinity checks passed. The 56-row normal-neighbor table consistently uses
HLA-B*50:01. Six applicable overlaps with the focused/historical tables agree
within floating-point tolerance. Model versions remain correlated checks, and
none of their scores or percentiles is a patient-specific probability. Choosing
leading peptides after screening 676 candidates also provides no calibrated
false-discovery rate. The overlapping BAP1 peptides remain one mutation lead.

Material presentation corrections completed at the parent's request:

1. The summary RNA_alt_fragments column is now RNA_variant_alt_fragments. All
   676 numeric/sequence/model rows are preserved exactly. A schema explains
   that BAP1's 254 supports the variant locus, while complete peptide-window
   RNA counts are 241 for the nine-mer and 245 for the eight-mer; none is an
   independent-UMI-molecule count.
2. The schema explicitly distinguishes separately optimized mutant/WT best
   alleles from fixed-allele comparisons. Ratios of their best affinities are
   not generally matched-HLA contrasts. Focused BAP1 B*50:01 contrasts already
   use the same allele correctly.

The source summary generator now reproduces the table from existing predictions
without rerunning a model, verifies preservation against the previous table,
and writes identical research-screen output and schema files to the delivery
directory. See candidate-screen-summary-generation.json for hashes.

Biological interpretation is appropriately bounded. Apcher's cell experiments
support the possibility of MHC-I peptide generation upstream of a premature
stop despite curtailed full-length protein production; they do not establish
production of this BAP1 peptide. Litchfield's observational frameshift/NMD
work supports investigation, not a mesothelioma vaccine success probability.
The existing stop-context audit reconstructs exon/junction distances without
claiming an NMD-escape fraction. Retain those limits.

Optional wording clarification for handoff: confirmation in an available
current tumor specimen need not imply that a new biopsy is mandatory. Suitable
existing material may answer the question; any additional biopsy is a clinical
decision. No evidence here establishes mutation presence at every current site,
tumor-cell HLA retention, protein presentation, T-cell recognition or benefit.
