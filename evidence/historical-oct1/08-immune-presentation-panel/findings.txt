Antigen-presentation and interferon pathway coding review
1 October 2026 — exploratory analysis of the July brain specimen

This bounded review did not identify a new obvious coding loss-of-function
candidate in the 17 selected genes. It does not establish intact immune
function or predict that immunotherapy or a vaccine will work.

Genes: B2M, TAP1, TAP2, TAPBP, JAK1, JAK2, IFNGR1, IFNGR2, STAT1, IRF1, NLRC5,
PSMB8, PSMB9, CIITA, RFX5, RFXANK and RFXAP. Conventional HLA-region variant
calling was deliberately excluded; HLA typing has a separate dedicated method.

Why this question matters

Primary research in melanoma linked specific acquired B2M/JAK1/JAK2 defects
to loss of antigen presentation or interferon signaling. Related work found
JAK1/2 inactivation in some primary-resistant melanoma/colorectal tumors.
Those experiments motivate looking for obvious defects; they do not make a
negative coding screen a positive response biomarker in mesothelioma.
https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1604958
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27903500/

What was done

One GENCODE37 selected protein-coding transcript per gene was reviewed, using
MANE, then APPRIS, then longest-CDS prioritization (16 MANE transcripts and
NLRC5 APPRIS principal_4), plus four bases around each
coding interval. This covered 34,273 distinct bases, 34,264 at >=20 strict
read depth. These are read depths, not unique-molecule counts or a validated
clinical detection limit. The nine lower-depth positions require their exact
coding-versus-flank context to be retained rather than claiming every base
was highly covered.

Initial filters were MAPQ/base quality >=20; no unmapped, duplicate, secondary,
supplementary or QC-failing reads. SNV candidates required >=10 alternate reads
and >=5% allele fraction. Small CIGAR indels additionally required >=10 paired
read names; this is a candidate screen, not a normalized clinical variant caller.
The existing source VCF was compared without treating its '.' filter as PASS.

There were 24 sequence candidates: 12 synonymous and 12 missense. No stop-gain
or frameshift met these thresholds. Exact forward-GRCh38 public mappings were
verified before attributing population frequencies to the observed alleles.
All 12 missense alleles occur at >=1% in at least one returned global public
population dataset. They are not promoted as newly discovered tumor-specific
immune-escape mutations. Population frequency by itself is not a complete
clinical pathogenicity assessment, and this patient has no matched normal in
the released files.

RNA context

All 17 genes have nonzero bulk RNA TPM in the supplied expression table.
B2M and JAK1 are abundant. Bulk expression cannot assign those transcripts to
malignant cells rather than immune/stromal/brain admixture and does not prove
correct protein localization, pathway signaling or peptide presentation.
No functional inference is made from an individual high or low TPM value.

Limits that matter for the research plan

This does not assess every transcript, structural rearrangement, copy-number
loss, promoter alteration, epigenetic silencing, subthreshold clone or other
immune-evasion mechanism. July pretreatment tissue cannot exclude resistance
that develops later or differences in current extracranial disease. A specific
HLA-B*50:01 peptide hypothesis still requires direct tumor-cell presentation
or functional recognition evidence; this screen does not waive that step.
