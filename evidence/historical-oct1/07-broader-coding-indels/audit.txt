PASS for the stated bounded research workflow. The computation is reproducible and appropriately conditional. These checks do not establish new somatic variants, clinical actionability, or validated neoantigens.

Computational validation
Thirty fully coding single-edit translations independently reproduce the original mutant protein sequence exactly, using full-mRNA coordinates and a fixed standard genetic-code table. All 32 reference proteins reproduce GENCODE37 translations. The two boundary cases, ZNF778 and ORAI1, are correctly withheld. This validation checks arithmetic and annotation projection; it does not validate a full expressed tumor protein.

All 24 original public catalog matches were independently reconstructed as exact alternate haplotypes against the local GRCh38 reference, with population frequencies tied to the matching allele. The subsequently resolved APOL4 rs5845253 and SLC27A3 rs572408453 matches also pass; SLC27A3 frequencies match the cached source response exactly. Eighteen shortlisted events have a returned global alternate frequency of at least 1%. APOL4 has no reliable frequency from the failed population lookup and must not be called common from that endpoint.

The supplied VCF contains 2,843 variant records. Of 32 shortlisted events, exact local haplotype comparisons locate BAP1, RASA1, and CHD2. BAP1 and RASA1 use FILTER="." (unspecified/unfiltered, not the literal string PASS); CHD2 uses R8.1, defined by the file as a monomer repeat length greater than 8. The other 29 events are absent as equivalent single exported alleles, allowing +/-70 bases of representation shift within the downloaded reference windows. This does not prove a laboratory miss or the export's clinical sensitivity.

Material caveat added by this audit
NLRC3 short-window reference reads are not an all-reference haplotype over the wider locus. A separate nearby missense allele is observed on those reads. This does not invalidate the insertion, whose alternate local sequence remains coherent, but longer haplotypes should be reconstructed before making allele-specific protein or peptide claims. The public rs773155391 record has a worst-consequence label of stop_gained, but the observed G>A substitution projects to c.1340C>T / p.Ser447Leu in ENST00000359128.10 (TCG to TTG). Do not apply record-wide labels to a specific allele.

The six events have credible local DNA evidence; RNA support varies from scant to moderate and includes repeated fragment endpoints. They are candidates for matched-normal and orthogonal confirmation, not validated tumor-specific targets. There is no evidence here to change cancer lineage or a treatment regimen. For future peptide work, normal-sample genotyping and patient-specific haplotype reconstruction take priority over generating more predictions from an unclassified alternate allele.

Scope and reproducibility
Scripts and audit-data.json preserve read counts, variant representations, transcript matches, public source matches, and bounded read evidence. Only the six requested loci were fetched from existing indexed BAMs. No full BAM rescan, patient-data upload, or clinical assay occurred. Wider exact-flank filters are a stress test; in RNA they can reduce assessability because of splice junctions and fragment boundaries, not necessarily biological absence. Endpoint signatures are a descriptive check, not a validated duplicate-removal method.
