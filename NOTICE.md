# Data and reuse notice

This private repository contains sensitive human genomic data and research predictions. Repository access and access to the linked raw-data Drive folder are managed separately. Do not publish or redistribute patient-derived sequences or findings without the family's permission and appropriate consent.

No general open-source or open-data license is granted by this repository. Bundled third-party methods retain their original notices and licenses. Scripts authored for this analysis are supplied for invited reviewers to inspect and reproduce this work; contact the maintainers for other reuse.

These are computational research analyses, not a validated diagnostic assay or prescribing guidance. Report versions, unresolved limitations and the distinction between observed reads and predicted biology are part of the evidence.
