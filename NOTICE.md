# Data and reuse notice

This public release includes patient-derived human genomic data, HLA/genotype information, read evidence and research predictions. The large original inputs are linked from external public storage. These materials remain sensitive and potentially identifying; an accession identifier does not anonymize genomic data. The repository is shared for inspection, replication and scientific scrutiny.

Public availability does not itself establish a blanket open-source or open-data license. No general license grant is created by this notice. Bundled third-party materials retain their original notices and licenses; obtain external software, references and model weights from their upstream sources and follow their terms. Contact the maintainers about reuse beyond the rights already granted by the relevant rights holders. Do not imply endorsement by Caris, a treating clinician, a research institution or a software author.

AI agents assisted code, execution, interpretation and writing. Historical “independent audit/review” labels denote internal computational cross-checks using separate implementations within that workflow, not outside peer review or laboratory confirmation. These are research analyses, not a validated diagnostic assay or prescribing guidance. Report versions, corrected terminology, unresolved limitations and the distinction between observed reads and predicted biology are part of the evidence.
