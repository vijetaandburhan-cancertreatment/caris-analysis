Patient analysis retains accepted/discarded calls, full fusion-transcript and peptide fields, read identifiers, Chimeric.out.junction, SJ.out.tab and all logs. An independently tested FIFO/tee preserves a byte-identical BAM stream to Arriba while C-backed samtools selects alignments overlapping BAP1, RASA1, LATS1 and LATS2. Mates outside these gene intervals are not guaranteed retained. The full local BAM is not stored because of disk limits.

Important bounds: this is genome-aware RNA analysis, not whole-genome DNA sequencing. Arriba does not call intragenic deletions, so a fusion-negative result cannot rule out BAP1/LATS exon loss. Short anchors and MAPQ255 require careful review of competing alignments; all candidates still need orthogonal/clinical validation. Sparse indexing and primary-assembly reference scope limit negative conclusions. Compact-control missing-contig warnings were preserved through verified lossless log compression, not suppressed by changing the biological caller.
